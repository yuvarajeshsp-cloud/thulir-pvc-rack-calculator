Read the build assumptions
  • Width and depth are measured between corner-post centrelines, not outside edges or clear tray space. Fitting bodies extend beyond these dimensions.
  • Clear rail gap is measured from one horizontal pipe’s top surface to the next pipe’s underside. Shelf mesh, lights, fittings and trays reduce usable crop clearance.
  • Stepped layout creates 1–6 separate full-depth rows rising backwards. Total depth equals row depth × step count; rise is measured between rail centrelines. Adjacent rows share posts, with separate fittings at each shelf height. Overhead frames are excluded in stepped mode; lights need separate mounting.
  • In vertical mode, every level uses four corner fittings. With centre support enabled, each level also uses two 5-way fittings and a central depth rail. Only the topmost four corners use 3-way elbows; all lower corners use 4-way tees.
  • Four separate vertical corner posts and two centre posts continue between levels. These are individual cut pipe segments, not pipes passed through fittings.
  • All sockets use your entered insertion depth. Centre-to-mouth dimensions are entered by fitting type; if individual outlets differ, measure and adjust those cuts separately.
  • The stock plan uses first-fit decreasing with one saw kerf per piece. It is an estimate, not a guaranteed minimum. Offcuts do not include end trimming or defective pipe.
  • Fan and LED quantities follow the enabled vertical-rack options. Irrigation uses one nozzle for each of the four illustrated trays per shelf. Equipment routes and mounts are indicative, not a wiring or hydraulic design.
  • Braces, wall restraints and glue are not automatically quantity-calculated; add them as custom estimate items. Check pipe wall thickness, shelf mesh support and wet-tray loads before building. This is a materials planner, not a structural load approval.
